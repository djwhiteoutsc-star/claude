# Excuse Cards — First Set (30)

## Battle rules
- The teacher starts a battle with **Suspicion** set by their type: Substitute 40, Hall Monitor 55, Coach 60, Principal 75. The meter runs from 0 to 100.
- You get **3 Composure** per turn and draw up to a hand of **5**.
- Each card lowers Suspicion by its **Reduction**.
- **Combo rule:** if the card played *just before this one, this turn,* has the tag in this card's **Combo From**, this card's Reduction is **doubled**.
- When you end your turn, the teacher adds **Pressure** to Suspicion: 10 at the start, plus 3 for each turn after that.
- **Suspicion 0** means you're released. **100** means detention.
- **Support** cards can be played by a nearby friend (Back Them Up).
