# HALL PASS — Steam Store Page (Draft)

## Capsule tagline
**Sneak out of class. Don't get caught. Have an excuse ready.**

## Short description (≤300 chars)
A 1–6 player co-op stealth comedy. Sneak through school mid-class to pull off ridiculous missions before the bell rings. Caught by a teacher? Battle their suspicion with a deck of Excuse Cards — or end up in detention waiting for your friends to bust you out.

---

## About This Game

### 🎒 SNEAK
The bell just rang and your squad has a list: swipe the confiscated phone from the principal's office, swap the cafeteria pudding, return the class hamster before anyone notices it's gone. Every school day reshuffles the halls, the classrooms and the missions. Crouch past windows, duck into empty desks to blend in, and keep your voice down — teachers can **hear** you.

### 🃏 GET CAUGHT
It's going to happen. When a teacher grabs you, their **Suspicion Meter** starts filling. Play Excuse Cards to talk it back down — *Nurse's Note*, *Bathroom Emergency*, *I Was Helping Mr. Davis* — and chain them into combos. Friends nearby can jump in with **Back Them Up** cards. Run out of excuses and it's straight to detention.

### 🍫 BUILD YOUR EMPIRE
Smuggled snacks are currency. Between school days, spend them on new excuses, silent sneakers, decoy phones, and grow your locker-trading business into the most powerful underground operation in the building.

## Features
- **1–6 player online co-op** (solo playable)
- **Proximity voice chat** — whisper to your squad, but too loud and the Hall Monitor hears it
- **Excuse Card battles** — a quick deckbuilding twist on getting caught
- **Randomized school days** — new layouts, objectives and teacher patrols every run
- **Four teachers, four playstyles** — the speedy Hall Monitor, sprinting Coach, oblivious Substitute and the dreaded Principal
- **Detention jailbreaks** — get your friends out before the period ends
- **Detention Cam** — automatic replay of the funniest catch every round
- **Yearbook awards** — "Most Likely to Get Caught by the Lunch Lady"
- **Tons of cosmetics** — hoodies, backpacks, skateboards, earned by playing. No microtransactions.

## Mature Content
None. No violence, no gore, no horror. Target rating: **E10+ / PEGI 7–12**.
